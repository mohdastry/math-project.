The Bioscience Scenario

                

                    In Malaysian agro-processing, Grey Oyster Mushrooms are vital for meat-alternative products and local fresh markets. To maximize yield, growers must know exactly when the biological growth rate peaks (the point of diminishing returns) to schedule harvests and optimize climate controls.
                

                

                    Data Context: Parameters are adapted from growth profiles observed under Malaysian ambient humidity (80-90%) and temperature (28°C-30°C) conditions using standard rubberwood sawdust bags.
                

            


            

                
Mathematical Model

                
We model the cumulative flush biomass weight $f(x)$ in grams over a 20-day fruiting cycle using a tailored Logistic Growth Function:

                
                

                    Biomass Function f(x):
                    

                        f(x) = 250 / (1 + e^(-0.4 * (x - 10)))
                    

                    
    
                        
  • x = Days since primordial pinhead formation.
    • 
                        
  • 250 = Maximum carrying capacity (grams per bag).
    • 
                        
  • 10 = Day of maximum acceleration (inflection point).
    • 
                    

                


                

                    Growth Rate Derivative f'(x):
                    

                        f'(x) = [100 * e^(-0.4 * (x - 10))] / [1 + e^(-0.4 * (x - 10))]^2
                    

                    

                        Calculates the instantaneous growth rate in grams per day at any exact moment.
